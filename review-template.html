01 急症正文和FAQ仍需优先修正

优先修改 · PDF第7—8、12页

Contact a veterinarian promptly if you see … difficulty breathing …
If the dog is choking and cannot breathe, seek emergency veterinary care immediately.
…contact a veterinarian promptly if any appear.

问题：呼吸困难已需紧急处理，不应等到完全不能呼吸。反复无效干呕、突然腹胀也不能只写成一般咨询。第12页答案把症状出现作为联络条件，遗漏“吞入大块或大小不明，即使看似正常也联系兽医”。这不是要求照抄上轮，而是处置边界仍未解决。

怎么改：保留第7页大标题；从“How you respond…”起至第8页“What Not to Do”末尾，整体换为下列三段。避免继续使用“Mild or Uncertain Signs”给未知吞咽风险贴上轻度标签。

Seek emergency veterinary care now if your dog has difficulty breathing, repeatedly tries to vomit without bringing anything up, or develops a suddenly swollen abdomen. Call the clinic while arranging transport. These signs can have several serious causes and do not confirm a rawhide blockage. [7]

Contact your veterinarian promptly if your dog swallows a large piece, or you suspect a piece was swallowed but cannot tell its size, even if your dog seems well. Report vomiting, reduced appetite, unusual lethargy, abdominal discomfort, or difficulty passing stool. [1][5]

Tell the veterinarian when it happened, the approximate size of the piece, and your dog's symptoms. Keep the product label available. Do not attempt home treatment or induce vomiting unless instructed. Do not put your fingers into a choking dog's mouth. Do not wait for the piece to pass before calling.

第12页吞咽FAQ完整替换

Seek emergency veterinary care for breathing difficulty, repeated unproductive retching, or a suddenly swollen abdomen. If a large piece was swallowed, or its size is uncertain, contact your veterinarian promptly even if your dog seems well. Do not attempt home treatment or induce vomiting unless instructed, and do not wait for the piece to pass before calling. [1][5][7]

参考[7]应换成已读取的common-emergencies-in-dogs路径，见第04项。VCA急症页的催吐警告原本位于中毒段；此处“不自行处理”是保守求助原则，不把它写成生皮异物专门试验结论。最终医疗处置文字建议兽医复核。

02 两张信息图都需要改文案

优先修改 · PDF第4、6页

第6页 不能把三个检查画成安全认证

具体问题：“the three things that keep a chew safe”承诺过强；“Longer than your muzzle”主语指向读者，且长度单一条件不能保证“can't go in one bite”；绿色对勾也容易被理解为尺寸大就通过安全检查。“Stay in the room”弱于主动观察。页脚“A chew that is too small or too hard is the one that causes trouble”过度归因。

怎么改：保留三栏、眼睛和手部图标。尺寸栏的绿色通过勾改成中性“Check fit”提示，图中不放统一英寸或嘴长比。按压图可以作为检查动作示意，但不能配通过即安全的结论。整张图的文字层用下面版本。

Title
Three checks before offering a chew

Subtitle
These checks help assess suitability. They do not eliminate risk.

1 Size
Choose a chew your dog cannot readily swallow whole. Recheck as it wears down.

2 Firmness
Avoid very hard chews. Ask your veterinarian which textures suit your dog's teeth.

3 Supervision
Watch actively. Remove loose pieces and small remnants.

Footer
Follow the product's directions and stop if your dog gulps pieces or shows discomfort.

Caption
Size, firmness, and active supervision are checks for suitability, not guarantees of safety.

正文同步：第4页牙齿段和第6页“Press it…should give slightly”不必包装为可验证的安全测试。第4页牙齿段改为：

Very hard chews can chip or fracture teeth. Ask your veterinarian which textures suit your dog's teeth and chewing behavior, especially if your dog has dental disease or damaged teeth. [3]

第6页按压句改为以下一句，其余个体尺寸提醒可保留：

Avoid very hard chews and ask your veterinarian about suitable textures; pressing a chew does not establish that it is safe. [3]

第4页 三步处置图不等于四类风险图

具体问题：图注“At a glance: the four risk groups and who is most exposed”与图像不符。“Swap it for a fresh chew”容易被理解为每次变小就再补一根，与热量预算冲突。“Watch for signs”也不能代替吞下大块后的主动求助。

怎么改：无需重画整套风格。保留手、垃圾桶和警示图标，修改文字，建议将图移到第7页“Supervision and Replacement”附近。使用下列完整文字层和图注：

Title
When to take the chew away

Subtitle
Remove loose pieces and the remainder before they can be swallowed whole.

1 Watch the chew
Stop if pieces break off or your dog starts gulping.

2 End the session
Remove loose pieces and discard the small remainder.

3 Respond promptly
If a large piece is swallowed, contact your veterinarian even if your dog seems well.

Footer
Breathing difficulty or repeated unproductive retching needs emergency veterinary care.

Caption
Remove loose or small pieces, end the session, and seek veterinary advice after suspected large-piece ingestion.

GEO与可访问性：图中文字也可能被读者或OCR单独摘录，不能比正文更绝对。上线时将关键安全结论同时保留为可选择的正文文字。PDF中这两张图的文字不在普通正文抽取结果里；这不证明所有AI都读不到图，但不能只依赖图片表达关键警示。

06 比较表需要区分材质与用途

PDF第3、8—9页

当前表格同时列出材质类别和用途类别，读者可能不易理解它们之间的重叠关系。“Dried meat or protein chews通常是单一原料”不能由类别名推定；“Dental chews是defined shape and texture”也不足以解释牙齿用途。硬尼龙属于非食用咀嚼玩具，应明确其用途。

怎么改：将第8—9页比较表整体替换为下表，并用表后英文段落替换原来的“Neither category…”及“Compare the individual product…”两段。表前导语可以保留，不要求把五行缩回两行。

Rawhide-freeLabeled as containing no rawhide; the rest of the formula varies.The full recipe, dietary restrictions, calories, size, texture, and swallowed-piece risk.
